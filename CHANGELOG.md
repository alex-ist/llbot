# Changelog

## 2026

- Added pronunciation practice with microphone recording, automatic recording stop, waveform visualization, IPA transcription, and detailed pronunciation scoring.
- Correct spoken answers can automatically flip the card.

## 2025

- Added IPA transcriptions and pronunciation audio from the Cambridge Dictionary.
- Improved AI-assisted word creation: part-of-speech detection, translations, example sentences, and example translations.
- Words can be entered in either English or Russian and translated automatically.
- Added support for editing and deleting saved words.

## 2024

- Added voice input and speech recognition for training answers.
- Added commands for opening the word library, adding words, and viewing help.
- Improved card ordering, training flow, and microphone handling.

## Core features

- Works as a Telegram Mini App inside Telegram.
- Track learning progress for every word.
- Review forgetting statistics after each training session.
- Open dictionary references directly from cards.
- Start with themed word sets.
